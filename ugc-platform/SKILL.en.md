# UGC platforms (ugc-platform)

Develop a concept for a platform with user-generated content: blogs, video hosting, social networks. The skill helps the product team design the author funnel, the system for monetizing authors (creators economy), and the rules for automatic moderation.

## Process
1. **Develop a creator funnel (Creator Funnel).** How to encourage users to create content (first views, easy editor tools).
2. **Design content moderation.** Detection of spam, pornography, hate speech (AI filters + crowdsourced reports + manual moderation).
3. **Author reward mechanisms.** Ad network (RevShare), donations, paid subscription to the author.
4. **Save the output** in the current working directory as `ugc-platform-[context].md`.

## Output Format
```
## UGC Platform Concept: [Name]
- **Content moderation:** two-step review (AI filter on upload -> manual handling of complaints by moderators).
- **Creator Economy:** payment to authors of 50% of the revenue from ads shown on their videos upon reaching 10,000 subscribers.
```
